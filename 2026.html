🛠️ Special Awards by Hardware Category

Both missions are open to competitors across 4 hardware categories (see project details for eligibility criteria):

  • 1. Open Platform: Open to robots using any controller brand
  • 2. INEX Category: For robots using INEX controllers exclusively
  • 3. Princebot Category: For robots using Princebot controllers exclusively
  • 4. LEGO Education: For robots using LEGO Education controllers exclusively

🏆 Awards & Scholarships

All participating teams will receive a medal-level certificate (Gold, Silver, or Bronze) based on scoring criteria for each mission.
📍 Regional Round

Certificates will be awarded to top-ranked teams in each Workshop group.

Teams ranked 1st–3rd in each Workshop group will receive a registration fee waiver for the National Final.
